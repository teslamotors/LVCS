# Tesla Standard MV (Mid-Voltage) External Support Connector

Similar to LVCS, we are standardizing our MV External Support interface for 48V (MV) vehicles in our continuing effort to simplify electrical connectivity requirements for all our vehicles. Outlined is the MV External Support interface used by our 48V vehicles and service operations and as with LVCS, this standardization unlocks further operational efficiencies, cost reductions and manufacturing automation.
